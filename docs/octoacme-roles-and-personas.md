# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA/Testing Lead owns the quality strategy for a project. They define testing standards, coordinate testing activities across the team, and act as the final quality gate before releases. They work closely with Developers to identify defects early and with Project Managers to incorporate testing time into the schedule.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the test strategy, including unit, integration, regression, and acceptance testing
- Review acceptance criteria written by Product Managers to ensure testability
- Coordinate QA activities and manage test environments
- Track defects, triage severity, and ensure resolution before release
- Report quality metrics and release readiness to stakeholders

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Establish repeatable, automated test coverage
- Provide clear go/no-go recommendations at release milestones

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups focused on defect status and blocking issues
- Test reports and sign-off documentation shared with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Direct collaboration with Developers during bug triage and root-cause analysis

---

## Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
The Sponsor or Executive Stakeholder provides organizational support, funding, and strategic direction for a project. They remove high-level blockers and ensure the project aligns with business priorities. They rely on Project Managers and Product Managers for regular status updates and escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Champion the project at the executive level and secure necessary resources
- Review and approve project scope, budget, and major milestone decisions
- Resolve cross-organizational blockers that cannot be handled at the team level
- Hold the team accountable to agreed outcomes and success metrics

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain organizational alignment on priorities and investment
- Minimize strategic risk and maximize return on project investment

### Typical Communication
- Executive status summaries (weekly or bi-weekly) from Project Managers
- Milestone review and steering committee meetings
- Escalation paths for scope changes, budget adjustments, and major risks

---

## Technical Lead / Solutions Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead or Solutions Architect is responsible for the overall technical direction of a project. They make key architectural decisions, establish engineering standards, and guide Developers in implementing solutions that are scalable, secure, and maintainable. They bridge technical execution with business requirements in collaboration with Product Managers and Business Analysts.

### Responsibilities
- Define the system architecture and technology choices
- Review and approve technical designs and significant pull requests
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies in coordination with Project Managers
- Mentor Developers and provide guidance on complex implementation challenges
- Ensure non-functional requirements (performance, security, scalability) are addressed

### Goals
- Deliver a coherent, maintainable technical solution
- Reduce architectural debt and long-term operational risk
- Align engineering decisions with product and business goals

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decision records (ADRs) and technical design documents
- Participation in sprint planning and backlog refinement to advise on feasibility
- Regular syncs with Product Managers and Project Managers on technical trade-offs

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst acts as the bridge between business stakeholders and the delivery team. They elicit, document, and validate requirements, ensuring that what is built matches what the business actually needs. They work closely with Product Managers on problem framing and with Developers and the Technical Lead on solution feasibility.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document detailed business and functional requirements
- Translate stakeholder needs into user stories, process flows, and acceptance criteria
- Facilitate workshops and interviews with subject-matter experts and the Sponsor
- Validate that delivered functionality meets the documented requirements
- Maintain requirements traceability throughout the project lifecycle

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are complete, unambiguous, and actionable
- Reduce rework caused by misunderstood or missing requirements
- Provide the team with clear documentation that supports both development and testing

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- User stories and process documentation shared with Product Managers, Developers, and the QA/Testing Lead
- Sign-off sessions with the Sponsor or business owners to confirm requirements accuracy
